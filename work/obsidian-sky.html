Overview

Self-initiated

Obsidian Sky imagines a landscape that keeps rewriting itself — a place where every return reveals a version of the memory that never happened. The film was built by generating in disciplined bursts: one location, one palette, one camera behaviour per sequence.

Generation was only the raw footage. The real work was editorial — selecting eleven usable frames from hundreds, stabilising motion, rebuilding continuity, grading everything into one cinematic base and adding sound design that gives synthetic images weight.

AI gives you infinite frames; the craft is choosing the eleven that matter.
Creative process

Four moves, every project

  1. 01

    Prompt direction

    Shot list, lens language and palette locked before generation to avoid model drift.

  2. 02

    Generation passes

    Hundreds of candidates generated, then filtered to a usable selects reel.

  3. 03

    Continuity fix

    Stabilisation, warp repair and re-timing so synthetic shots behave like real footage.

  4. 04

    Grade & sound

    Unified cinematic grade plus designed ambience and a sparse score.
