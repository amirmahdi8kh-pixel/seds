Overview

Rova Sportswear

Rova drops a product every fortnight and needed reels that read instantly with the sound off. The system uses three fixed beats — hook, proof, payoff — with type large enough to survive a thumbnail and timing tight enough to survive a swipe.

Each drop ships as a set: a hero reel, three cutdowns and a still frame pulled from the same timeline, so the campaign stays coherent across feed, stories and paid placements.

In vertical video, the first frame is the headline and the second is the hook.
Creative process

Four moves, every project

  1. 01

    Hook design

    Three opening patterns tested against real retention data from the brand account.

  2. 02

    Beat system

    Hook, proof, payoff — fixed durations so every drop feels like part of a series.

  3. 03

    Sound-off type

    Oversized kinetic captions carrying the full message with audio muted.

  4. 04

    Rapid delivery

    A project template that turns a new drop into eight exports in under an hour.
